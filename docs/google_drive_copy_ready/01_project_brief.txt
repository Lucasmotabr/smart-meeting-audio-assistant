Project Brief - Smart Meeting Audio Assistant

PROJECT GOAL

We are building a real-time microphone-based dashboard.

The system will show:

- sound activity
- waveform
- spectrogram
- noise classification
- speech transcription
- audio quality
- AI advice

FINAL PIPELINE

Microphone Input
to Audio Stream
to Voice Activity / Waveform / Spectrogram
to Noise Classification
to Speech Recognition
to Audio Quality
to AI Advice
to GUI Dashboard

WEEK 1 GOAL

Each module works separately.
No full integration is required yet.

WEEK 2 GOAL

Connect all modules into the GUI.

WEEK 3 GOAL

Polish the demo and prepare the final presentation.

FINAL DEMO SCENARIOS

1. Clean speech
2. Keyboard typing noise
3. Far distance speech
4. Microphone comparison
