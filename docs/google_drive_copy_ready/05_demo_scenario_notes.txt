Demo Scenario Notes

For Week 1, this folder can stay simple.
Add materials only if someone already has them.

Scenario 1: Clean Speech

Expected result:
- high audio quality
- speech detected
- transcript is accurate
- AI advice says the audio condition is good

Scenario 2: Keyboard Typing Noise

Expected result:
- typing noise detected
- quality becomes medium or lower
- AI advice recommends reducing typing or moving closer to the microphone

Scenario 3: Far Distance Speech

Expected result:
- lower signal strength
- transcript may become less accurate
- AI advice recommends moving closer to the microphone

Scenario 4: Microphone Comparison

Expected result:
- compare MEMS microphone and condenser microphone
- show difference in signal quality and recognition stability
